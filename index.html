Environmental Remote Sensing Laboratory (ERSL) – The University of Alabama

Department of Geography & the Environment, The University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL 35487. Directed by Dr. Hongxing Liu. Advancing multi-scale Earth observation, UAV LiDAR and multispectral sensing, GeoAI, flood inundation mapping, water quality remote sensing, river morphology, and hydrologic modeling.

Research Themes

  • Water Quality Remote Sensing (Chlorophyll-a, Turbidity, Suspended Sediment, Sentinel-2, Sentinel-3 OLCI)
  • Flood Mapping (SAR, Optical Imagery, High-Resolution DEMs, Hydrologically Guided Deep Learning)
  • GeoAI & Machine Learning (SAM2, Spatially Transferable Deep Learning, Cloud Computing)
  • River Morphology (UAV LiDAR, Airborne Bathymetry, ADCP Surveys, Fluvial Dynamics)
  • Water Quantity Remote Sensing (NASA SWOT Altimetry, Reservoir Storage, Lake Levels, Discharge)

Sponsored Research & Collaborations

Supported by NASA, NOAA CIROH, Water Research Foundation (WRF), USGS, and the Alabama Centre of Excellence for inland, coastal, and estuarine water monitoring.
